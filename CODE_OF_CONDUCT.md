# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TwinLab2 to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Look after the data: never share real or personal data in issues, pull requests or recordings.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their
  explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, through a private report on the
repository's **Security** tab (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) if nothing else fits. Every report
is reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code and issues that break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban a participant,
temporarily or for good.
